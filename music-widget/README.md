# 🎵 Caelestia Music Widget

A beautiful, minimal and animated **MPRIS music widget for Quickshell**, designed to fit seamlessly with **Caelestia** and **Hyprland**.

The widget displays your currently playing music with a circular, spinning CD-style album cover, playback controls, seek bar, player switching, dynamic Caelestia colors, and smooth pop-in/pop-out animations.

## ✨ Features

* 🎵 **MPRIS support** — works with MPRIS-compatible media players
* 💿 **Circular CD album art**
* 🔄 **Spinning album art** while music is playing
* ▶️ **Play / Pause controls**
* ⏮️ **Previous track**
* ⏭️ **Next track**
* 🎚️ **Interactive seek bar**
* ⏱️ **Current time and track duration**
* 🎨 **Dynamic Caelestia theming**
* 🌈 Automatically follows `scheme.json`
* 🎭 Smooth pop-in and pop-out animations
* 🫧 Neumorphic-style shadows and lighting
* 🎚️ Adjustable card opacity
* 📐 Adjustable card position
* 🔒 **Lockable position**
* 💾 Position persistence across restarts
* 🎧 **Multiple**
